# Spec — Welsh marks: North Wales becomes region 'W'

## Why
The follow-on the Wales groundwork existed for. The regulatory spine is live
(specs/wales-groundwork-regs.md): saved Byelaw 19 sizes, bass dual-sourced, the
Dee-mouth boundary, the #sizes Wales block. Welsh marks are now pure venue-contract
work — this item ships the region.

## Spec-time feasibility (done 30 Jul 2026 — sources fetched raw, in scratchpad)
TurnersTackle — the source class that carried Fylde — has a full **North Wales
section**. Three pages are ver-grade (first-person, stats-rich, unsoftened hazards),
and each embeds its OWN tide widget naming a station:

- **Talacre** (turnerstackle.co.uk …/north-wales/talacre/) — low-water Dee-mouth
  beach at Point of Ayr. Window: 2h either side of LOW. Smoothhounds late Apr/early
  May, "mid May is when they arrive in numbers". Flatties + small bass at low;
  winter whiting/cod "but there are much easier and safer venues". Hazards, all the
  source's own words: soft sinking sand (darker sand = sinking), ~25 min walk each
  way, "the tide comes in quick so its a dangerous venue", fog "treacherous", night
  = don't fish alone, free car park locks evenings AND floods on big tides (40 cars
  written off 2016). "Quite a tidal pull on larger tides, being at the end of the
  river Dee" → heavier grippers. Parking CH8 9RP. Widget: **Mostyn Docks**
  ("its within a few minutes of Mostyn"). **Dual-sourced**: seaangler.co.uk
  Talacre mark page corroborates species (dab, whiting, codling, flounder, plaice,
  bass), seasons (Apr–Jun plaice/silver eels/school bass/flounder; late Oct–Feb
  dab/flounder/whiting/codling), "beware of incoming tide", green-light directions.
- **Splash Point, Rhyl** (…/north-wales/splash-point-rhyl/) — easy prom mark,
  flood venue "2 hours up 2 down", pay-and-display 30 s from the rails, sandy and
  snag-free "avoiding the groynes and sea defence rocks", tackle shops close by
  (none named). Summer bass (small, 1–2 lb) + flatfish; winter whiting/codling;
  smoothhounds May–Aug at low; species list includes **Lesser Weever** — the
  guide's weever first-aid gets a second venue-sourced anchor. Lug is the local
  bait. Parking LL18 3AY. Widget: **Mostyn Docks**.
- **Colwyn Bay** (…/north-wales/colwyn-bay/) — "one of if not the easiest venue
  for fishing in North Wales… fishing is possible from the boot of the car",
  "perfect for less able anglers". Railed prom, high-and-low mark fished mainly at
  HW, sandy, 4 oz grippers hold "unless there is a strong wind up", 2-hook flapper
  fine at HW. Mackerel shoals last two weeks of Aug + first week of Sept; summer
  dogfish/plaice/dab/school bass + lesser weevers; winter "a Whiting fest" +
  codling/rockling. Prime spot "said to be just by the arches" (hearsay inside a
  ver source → that sub badges anec). LL29 8ED, 30 s off the A55. Widget:
  **Colwyn Bay**.

Regulatory fit (already sourced, nothing new to fetch): all three sit on the open
North Wales coast / seaward side of the Dee-mouth line, so saved Byelaw 19 sizes
apply and bass is 42 cm national — the #sizes Wales block covers them as written.
Eels at Welsh marks are a CITED release (NRW byelaw is sea-inclusive), not the
guide's conservation call — the one place the eel rule is cleaner in Wales.

## Decisions (made now, at spec time)
- **Decision 0 — three marks, ship what passes.** Talacre, Splash Point Rhyl,
  Colwyn Bay, all region:'W', each rev-dated at build. The contract's floor stands:
  fewer than 2 passing marks = the region does not ship. Three candidates is
  deliberate margin, not scope creep.
- **Decision 1 — region lists gain North Wales.** Title, eyebrow, footer, offline
  SVG map, and a fourth mapjump button. First-visit "Where will you fish?" gains
  ['North Wales — Rhyl to Colwyn', colwynbay] — the representative venue is the
  one its source calls the easiest, matching the card's beginner audience.
  This reverses the groundwork's Decision 0 exactly as that decision said it would.
- **Decision 2 — regs stay centralised.** Each Welsh venue carries one stats row
  ['Rules','Welsh side — see "Fishing the Welsh side?" under Can I keep it?']
  linking #sizes (the anchor the groundwork shipped for this). No regulatory
  sentence is duplicated at venue level. BNA geometry: all three are expected
  OUTSIDE the 1990 Order's Hilbre Point → Mostyn Quay line; the build verifies
  against the Order's schedule (already in scratchpad) before writing any BNA
  word into venue copy — if ambiguous for Talacre, say the conservative thing
  and say why.
- **Decision 3 — smoothhound ships as species rows only, no fish-ID card.** The
  hound is the headline species at two marks (venue-sourced May–Aug windows), so
  it earns venue species bands and blurb mentions. An ID card needs its own
  sourced description work — that's a follow-on item, triggered now that shipped
  venues name the species. Same for thornback ray and bull huss (occasional
  mentions only).
- **Decision 4 — Decision A on RUN holds.** No source gives a run figure. Talacre's
  "quite a tidal pull on larger tides" is qualitative and ships as blurb/hazard
  prose with its consequence (heavier grippers), never as a RUN figure.
- **Decision 5 — tide scales from each page's own widget** (the Fylde rule):
  Mostyn Docks for Talacre and Splash Point, Colwyn Bay for Colwyn Bay. Never
  geographic.
- **Decision 6 — the tide-safety box gains one Talacre line.** A mark whose own
  source says "the tide comes in quick so its a dangerous venue if you don't allow
  yourself enough time to get off the beach", with a 25-minute exit and fog risk,
  registers in the safety box, not just the venue panel. Proportionate and
  prominent.
- **Decision 7 — shops: the Fylde policy.** Rhyl's source says shops are close by
  but names none; Colwyn's names towns, not shops. Nothing is listed until a named
  source states a shop. One candidate: SeaAngler's Llanddulas mark page reportedly
  names two Colwyn Bay shops — the build fetches it raw; if it names them, they
  ship attributed to it; if not, the no-shops line.
- **Decision 8 — one anec candidate, fetched at build or dropped silently.** A
  WSF forum thread reportedly gives "low water spring, Hilbre 2 m or smaller" as
  the tide-size selector for reaching Talacre's channel. If the build's raw fetch
  grounds it, it ships as an anec-badged stat; if not, it is dropped without
  softening (silent beats invented).

## Change
1. **VENUES** — three new entries after the Fylde block, full contract shape
   (name/chip/lat/lng/conf:'ver'/region:'W'/rev/why/blurb/stats/hazard/subs/fit/
   shops/species). Stance-level pins verified against satellite imagery at build
   (the Decision-A retry rule). All four fit verdicts per mark with whys traceable
   to sourced statements — where the source is silent the why says so honestly
   (e.g. Colwyn's sourced 4 oz gripper sits at a light rod's ceiling → light:'y'
   with that why).
2. **Region plumbing** — title, eyebrow, footer region lists; jumpW button +
   wiring mirroring jumpN/F/S; offline-SVG fallback gains a NORTH WALES row;
   first-visit fourth region option per Decision 1.
3. **Season calendar legend** — gains the venue-sourced windows (Talacre hounds
   mid-May+, SeaAngler Apr–Jun / late Oct–Feb lists, Rhyl hounds May–Aug, Colwyn
   mackerel late Aug–early Sept). Rows stay synthesised estimates; the
   table-level estimate tag stays where it is.
4. **Tide-safety box** — one Talacre line per Decision 6.
5. **"Who sets them" scope check** — the regs panel sentence was written when
   "the Welsh side" meant only the Dee bank. If its wording doesn't already cover
   open-coast North Wales marks, the minimal edit that makes it accurate — no new
   claims, checked against the already-fetched sources.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every venue stat, hazard, window, season and fit-why maps line-by-line to a raw
  build-time fetch of the named source (claim→source table in the build report).
- Tide-table stats name the venue page's own widget station; per-venue rev = the
  real build date; no venue inherits an older date.
- ≥2 marks pass the contract or the region does not ship; ≥1 ver (expected: 3).
- Size table, regs panel facts, and the Wales block byte-identical except the
  Decision-2/5 pointer rows and any "Who sets them" minimal edit.
- Chips reach 13 venues + all = 14: note in errata that the chip-grouping trigger
  is now met, as a candidate next item — do NOT build grouping here.
- Hash/share/catch-log accept the new keys with zero code change (they derive
  from VENUES) — asserted, not assumed.
- Four surfaces verified: JS-off, offline (new pins degrade to the SVG row),
  night, print (session sheet carries a Welsh venue correctly — build via
  sessionSheet(), never #printbtn).
- Size budget ≤ ~8 KB.

## Out of scope
Chip grouping (now triggered — its own item) · smoothhound/thornback/huss fish-ID
cards (follow-on, now unlocked) · Llanddulas, Prestatyn, Pensarn, Mostyn,
Connah's Quay (Turners pages exist — future candidates; Mostyn/Connah's Quay
would exercise the inner-Dee bass-42-only nuance) · Anglesey/Llŷn/Barmouth ·
Welsh-language content · any change to NWIFCA-sourced values or existing venues.

## Decisions & errata (post-build)

- **AMENDMENT to the feasibility section — the Talacre boundary claim was wrong, and it
  is the most important thing the build found.** The spec asserted all three marks "sit
  on the open North Wales coast / seaward side of the Dee-mouth line, so saved Byelaw 19
  sizes apply." Build-time reading of Turners kills that for Talacre: the fishing walk is
  "head to the right until just after the green bouy" — east, into the river mouth — and
  the saved byelaws' district boundary terminates at *the disused lighthouse at Point of
  Ayr*, which is the lighthouse standing on Talacre beach itself (OSM's lighthouse node
  and the pin coordinate are the same point; confirmed on satellite imagery, tower and
  shadow visible on the sand). Which side of that line a given stance sits on is not
  something the source, a map pin, or this guide can settle. Decision 2 pre-authorised
  the conservative path and it shipped: the Wales block now says the line ends on that
  beach, that a Dee-mouth mark can sit either side of it, and — the part that makes it
  harmless — **the byelaw's figures and the table's figures are the same figures, and
  bass is 42 cm either way. Only the signature changes.** No side is asserted.
  Same failure shape as the previous item's review catch (a boundary/absence claim taken
  further than the source supports), caught one phase earlier this time.
- **AMENDMENT to Decision 1 — the first-visit North Wales button lands on Rhyl, not
  Colwyn Bay.** The spec picked Colwyn because its source calls it the easiest venue on
  the coast, but that is a statement about *access*, not tackle. Colwyn's only sourced
  lead figure is "gripper leads from 4 ounce" (113 g) and more with wind — past both the
  light and bass rods once the 12 g rig allowance comes off, so `light` is honestly `r`.
  A beginner default must not open on a red verdict. Rhyl states no lead figure and no
  distance requirement, is snag-free sand at close range off the prom rails, 30 seconds
  from the car, with small fish — so it is both the better beginner default and the more
  defensible one. Reason recorded as a code comment at the button.
- **Decision 7 fired, and the shops were dropped.** Sea Angler's Llanddulas page does
  name two Colwyn Bay tackle shops with phone numbers, as the spec predicted. They were
  NOT shipped: the listing is dated 16 June 2021, it belongs to a different mark, neither
  shop has an OSM record, and nothing available confirms either still trades. Sending a
  beginner to a closed shop for bait is the same class of error as a false stamp. All
  three marks therefore carry the Fylde no-shops line, each phrased to what its own
  source actually says (Rhyl: shops close by, unnamed; Colwyn: towns named, no shop;
  Talacre: neither source names one).
- **Decision 8 dropped, silently, as written.** The WSF thread carrying the "low water
  spring, Hilbre 2 m or smaller" tide selector is behind a proof-of-work bot challenge.
  Solving it is off-limits, so the claim is ungrounded and simply does not appear —
  no softened version, no "reportedly". Recorded here so a later retry knows why.
- **Talacre stays `conf:'ver'`; the shifting ground rides a `shift` sub-spot.** The badge
  axis is attestation (how well-attested), not danger — collapsing danger into it would
  flatten two axes the way a fourth attestation badge would. Talacre is genuinely
  dual-sourced, so `ver` is the honest badge; the moving Dee-mouth sand gets its own
  `shift`-badged sub-spot, which is exactly the granularity Rock Channel already uses.
  The danger itself lives unsoftened in `hazard` and now also in the tide-safety box.
- **A cross-surface trap avoided by design, not by luck:** `sessionSheet()` runs stat
  values through `esc()`, so the Decision-2 Rules pointer had to be PLAIN TEXT — a link
  there would have printed as literal `&lt;a href…` markup on paper. Noted in the
  VENUES comment so the next venue does not reintroduce it.
- **The regs panel carried a claim this item made false.** "Every mark in this guide sits
  on the English shore" was true when written and is not any more. Rewritten to scope by
  border (Cumbria/Fylde/Merseyside + Thurstaston = NWIFCA; Talacre/Rhyl/Colwyn = Welsh
  Ministers and NRW). No regulatory fact changed — a sentence-level diff of the panel
  shows only the scoping sentences moving; the ten size-table rows are byte-identical to
  `HEAD` by regex extraction.
- **Verification:** 70 venue claims machine-matched against 5 raw build-time fetches,
  0 fails; 10 negative/silence claims (no shop named, no lead figure at Rhyl, no lure at
  Talacre or Colwyn, no cut-off hazard in Colwyn's source, no RUN figure) checked against
  article-body slices so site chrome could not create false positives, 0 fails. Browser
  assertions 31/31 after two harness bugs of my own were corrected — CSS `text-transform`
  uppercases `innerText`, and a `/wind/` regex matches "Window", which briefly looked like
  a weather-on-paper failure and was not. Pins verified against satellite imagery
  individually: Talacre lands on the lighthouse tower, Rhyl on the prom exactly where the
  sea-defence rock armour starts and the groynes run onto sand, Colwyn corrected from
  53.2952 to 53.2954 after imagery showed the first coordinate on the railway verge rather
  than the prom parking strip the source fishes from.
- **Order:** the Wales block was moved to the END of VENUES (after West Bank) so chips run
  Cumbria → Fylde → Mersey/Dee → North Wales, matching the jump buttons and the offline
  SVG. Done as a scripted move verified by character-multiset conservation; it needed one
  added comma, since West Bank had been the last entry.
- **Deviation — size budget.** +12,604 bytes (≈12.3 KB) against a "≤ ~8 KB" budget. The estimate was
  simply too low: three contract-shape venues with sourced blurbs, unsoftened hazards and
  four whys apiece cost ~3.5 KB each before any plumbing. Same trade as the previous item
  — trimming sourced venue content to hit a soft byte target is the wrong call — so the
  overshoot is declared rather than absorbed.
- **DEVIATION from an approved acceptance criterion — stated plainly, not buried.** The
  criterion said the Wales block stays "byte-identical except the Decision-2/5 pointer rows
  and any 'Who sets them' minimal edit." It does not: four sentences were added to it,
  carrying the Talacre boundary resolution above. Decision 2 pre-authorised the *substance*
  ("if ambiguous for Talacre, say the conservative thing and say why"), but the criterion as
  written forbade the edit, and the criterion is what was approved. Flagging it rather than
  letting "it was covered by a Decision" stand in for approval.
- **Stamp scope corrected on that new text.** The first draft said the boundary line "ends
  at the disused lighthouse standing on Talacre beach itself" under a stamp naming only the
  byelaw text and gov.wales bass guidance. The byelaw *names* the lighthouse; that it stands
  on this particular beach came from the OSM lighthouse node and the Esri imagery. Same shape
  as the two misattributions the previous item's review caught. Now the clause points readers
  at the satellite view (the guide's own idiom for reading ground) and the stamp says
  explicitly: "Where that lighthouse physically stands is read off the satellite imagery, not
  off the byelaw — the byelaw names it, it does not map it."
- **Session sheet measured, not assumed.** In the real print context (`body.session` applied)
  every mark fits one page and the hazard survives every cut — it carries no `data-cut` key,
  so `fitSheet` cannot reach it. The Welsh marks are the tightest in the file: Talacre and
  Colwyn Bay land at **1021 px of 1039 px**, against 782–881 px for the existing marks. They
  fit, but there is ~18 px of headroom, so the next verbose mark should measure before it
  ships rather than after.
- **Pre-existing defect found while measuring — NOT fixed here, offered as its own commit.**
  `fitSheet()` runs from the print button *before* `body.session` is added, so it measures the
  sheet in screen typography (~1188 px for Hodbarrow) against the print page height (1039 px).
  It therefore concludes the sheet overflows on **every** venue and drops all three cuttable
  blocks — tackle shops, sub-spots and the rules panel — every single time, then prints
  "Trimmed to one page: …" even though the real post-cut height is 782 px, leaving ~257 px
  unused. Consequence worth naming: the previous item's `sessionSheet()` fix (`querySelector`
  → `querySelectorAll`, so both regs paragraphs reach the sheet) is real but never reaches
  paper, because the whole `data-cut="regs"` block is removed before printing. Reproduced on
  Hodbarrow, untouched by this item, so it is not a regression from these marks. Out of scope
  here per the one-item-per-commit rule.
- **Confirmed negative — no size row for smoothhound, dogfish or huss.** Smoothhound is the
  headline fish at two of the three new marks, so this was checked rather than assumed:
  neither the saved Byelaw 19 text nor the NWIFCA size schedules set a minimum for hound,
  dogfish or huss (NWIFCA's only "Smooth-hound" hit is a navigation item for a Wirral
  research page, not a size). Nothing to add to the table, and no gap introduced — Perch Rock
  already shipped Smoothhound with no size row on the English side.
- **First-visit and map jumps re-verified after the change:** the card renders with five
  where-buttons, "North Wales — Rhyl to Colwyn" selects Rhyl, the panel opens on Splash Point
  with a green light-rod verdict (the point of the Decision 1 amendment), the card clears once
  both questions are answered, and the rig chain paints and repaints on rod change. `jumpW`
  frames all three Welsh marks, and jumpN/F/S still frame theirs.
- **Chip-grouping trigger is now met:** 13 marks + all = 14 chips. Not built here (out of
  scope); it is the strongest candidate for the next item.
- Four surfaces verified: JS-off (all 10 new static-markup sites asserted present outside
  the `<script>` block), offline (zero new network origins vs `HEAD`; the offline SVG
  gained a North Wales row and its viewBox grew 300→320 so the legend is not clipped),
  night (new panel flips correctly, no horizontal overflow from the long parking string),
  print (`beforeprint` still strips night; sheets build for all three marks with no markup
  leak, and no weather verdict reaches paper while the tides block still does).

### Review findings (Fable, 31 Jul 2026) — verdict PASS after one in-item fix

- **Independent re-fetch of all six sources** (fresh copies, own strip pass, claims
  re-derived from the shipped HTML, not the build's list): **67/67 positive claims and
  10/10 negative claims PASS**, including all verbatim quotes, both Mostyn Docks widget
  embeds and the Colwyn Bay one, and the Llanddulas drop rationale (the page really
  does name both shops, really is dated 16 June 2021). All 14 diff hunks map one-to-one
  to the declared change list; LEADS/REELBAND/GAPREEL/SEGS/VWORD/clsOf/rodOf/SEGWIRED/
  canonical all untouched; size table byte-identical throughout.
- **The review catch: both new 8-stat venues overflowed the session sheet in the
  worst gear state.** The build measured 1021 px and called it fits — but sheet height
  varies with the rig (the setup block and shock-leader line are gear-driven), and at
  beach/braid15 Talacre and Colwyn Bay measured **1050 px against the 1039 px page,
  post-every-cut**, meaning even the future fitSheet fix could never save them: with
  shops, subs and regs all dropped they still spilled ~11 px onto page two — under a
  footer reading "Trimmed to one page". Root cause is structural: 8 stat rows where
  every shipped venue carries 6, and the sheet's 3-column grid pays a whole row for
  the overflow. **Fix: regrouped both venues to 6 stats — Walk merged into Window and
  Lead into Ground (Talacre), Lead into Ground and Access into Parking (Colwyn) — no
  fact dropped, no safety wording touched.** Re-measured worst-case across every
  rod × line combination in the real print context: Talacre 990, Rhyl 991, Colwyn
  1010, all under 1039; existing venues 812–911. Every merged fact was already in the
  claim matcher, so the pass count is unchanged. Rhyl keeps its 8 rows because it
  fits; row-count uniformity is not a review criterion.
- **The lesson, promoted to the venue contract: a sheet fit is a measurement, not an
  eyeball, and it must be taken worst-case** — `body.session` applied, across rod/line
  combos — because `fitSheet` alone measures screen typography and lies (the
  pre-existing defect the build documented).
- **The Rhyl analogue of the Talacre boundary question was checked and resolves
  cleanly.** The byelaw's district also stops at "a line drawn across the river Clwyd,
  along the seaward side of the bridge carrying the A548 road at Rhyl" — the build
  never read that clause. Splash Point sits on the open coast ~1.6 km east of the
  Clwyd mouth; the exclusion trims only the river upstream of the bridge, and no
  shipped Rhyl copy makes a per-venue byelaw claim anyway. Nothing to change — but
  the check is recorded because an unread boundary clause is exactly how the last
  item's absence-claim failure started.
- **Confirmed negatives re-confirmed from my own fetches:** no smoothhound, dogfish or
  huss minimum in the saved Byelaw 19 text or on a fresh NWIFCA sizes fetch (NWIFCA's
  only "Smooth-hound" hit is a research-page nav item). The build's imagery-scoped
  stamp sentence is accurate: the byelaw names "the disused lighthouse at the Point of
  Ayr, in the county of Flintshire" and maps nothing.
- Browser re-verification from a clean profile: no hash on fresh load, 13 chips + all,
  contract fields on all three venues, sun times for Talacre within 2 minutes of New
  Brighton (0.3° apart — sane), beforeprint strips night, hazard/badges/Rules pointer
  on every Welsh sheet, no weather verdict on paper. Final delta +12,489 bytes
  (the review regroup recovered ~150 bytes; the ~8 KB budget deviation stands as
  declared). Test-state cleanup done (profile key removed, hash cleared).
